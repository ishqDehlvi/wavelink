What we heard

The brief

An open-source, self-hosted connector that brings a business owner's WhatsApp into ChatGPT and Claude. The mark must feel friendly and direct, never imitate WhatsApp or Meta, and work as a 16 px favicon.

directfriendlytrustworthyopen

Success: reads as W for Wavelink, owns a wave without a speech bubble or phone, holds at 16 px, works in one colour.

Wavelink — final identity2
Final design

Wave-W

One continuous wave that draws a W, with a dot as the message it carries.

  • The W and the wave are the same stroke: the name and the idea in one gesture
  • The dot is the link: a message leaving the wave for the AI on the other side
  • Built from 75° arms and two radii; a heavier small cut keeps it clear at 16 px
  • No speech bubble or phone, so it never reads as WhatsApp
