# Code of conduct

Everyone taking part in this project, in issues, discussions, pull requests and chat, agrees to:

- Be respectful. Disagree with ideas, not people.
- Assume good faith, and ask before concluding.
- Keep private things private. Never post other people's messages, numbers or names from WhatsApp, even in bug reports.
- Accept decisions by the maintainers about scope and direction, and argue for changes with reasons rather than pressure.

Harassment, discrimination, doxxing and sustained disruption are not tolerated and lead to removal from the project spaces.

To report a problem, contact the maintainer, [Inzemam](https://www.inzemam.tech/), through the details on that site or on the [GitHub profile](https://github.com/ishqDehlvi), or use GitHub's report feature. Reports are handled privately.

This document follows the spirit of the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/), version 2.1.
